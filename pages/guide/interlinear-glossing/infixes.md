# Infixes

An infix sits inside another morpheme. Write it in angle brackets, `b<um>ili`, and its gloss in angle brackets too. The infix's gloss goes before the gloss of the morpheme it interrupts when the infix is near that morpheme's start (`<ACTFOC>buy`), and after it when the infix is near the end (`leave<PRS>`). The other glosses follow their morphemes in order.

This page builds on [Morphemes and morphemic glosses](#guide-morphemes-and-glosses). Its examples are those of the Leipzig Glossing Rules.

```gloss "Tagalog. Leipzig Glossing Rules, example 27."
\abbr ACTFOC actor focus

\igt
\text bumili
  \morph b<um>ili
\glmorph <ACTFOC>buy
\ft buy
```

```gloss "Latin. Leipzig Glossing Rules, example 28."
\igt
\text relinquere
  \morph reli<n>qu-ere
\glmorph leave<PRS>-INF
\ft to leave
```

With morpheme breaks shown, the infix is drawn in its brackets. The two parts of *bili* still count as one morpheme, so a stem or root marked on it covers both. A root can run across an infix, which isn't part of it: `(b<um>ili)`, or `(b)<um>(ili)` (see [Stems and roots](#guide-stems-and-roots)).

An infix can also sit in a copied part (see [Morphemes and morphemic glosses](#guide-morphemes-and-glosses) for `~`):

```gloss "Tagalog. Leipzig Glossing Rules, example 31."
\abbr ACTFOC actor focus

\igt
\text bumibili
  \morph b<um>i~bili
\glmorph <ACTFOC>IPFV~buy
\ft is buying
```
