# Informal glosses

`\glword` gives each word a plain translation of its own, its informal gloss:

```gloss
\igt
\text Mūsa, mihī causās memorā
  \morph Mūs-a mihī caus-ās memor-ā
\glmorph muse-VOC.SG 1SG.DAT cause-ACC.PL recount-2SG.PRS.ACT.IMP
\glword {O Muse} {to me} {the causes} recount
```

- Write one [entry](#guide-tiers-and-rows) per word. Braces group an entry of several words, `{O Muse}`, and `{}` is an empty one.
- Any characters can be written in it, and each entry can be formatted on its own: `{**the** causes}` (see [Formatting and links](#guide-formatting-and-links)). Unlike in the translation, a formatting character there is always formatting, so escape one you want as it is: `{2 \* 3}`.
- It doesn't show where morphemes divide; that is what the morphemic gloss is for (see [Morphemes and morphemic glosses](#guide-morphemes-and-glosses)).

In *Display options* (the *Glossing* group), *Informal gloss* shows or hides it.
