# Saving and exporting

Your texts and your edits are saved in this browser, on this device, and nowhere else, so export anything you want to keep. An exported text can be imported again, here or in another browser.

## Your texts

- *New*, under *My texts* or on any text's page, starts a blank text or imports a file.
- *Duplicate* copies any text, published or your own, into *My texts*.
- *Delete* removes one of your own texts. It takes two clicks.

## Editing a published text

Editing a published text keeps your version in this browser; the published text doesn't change. While you have a version of your own, the text's page says so, and *Discard my changes* goes back to the published text.

If the published text changes after you have started editing it, the page says that too. *Keep my version* keeps yours and stops the notice; *Discard my changes* shows the new one.

## Export and import

- *Export*, on a text's page, saves the text as a plain-text file (`.gloss.txt`), in the same format you write. Its first line, `\glosshubformat 2`, says which version of the format it uses (see [Special characters and other quirks](#guide-special-characters)); you can leave it out when writing a text yourself.
- *Export everything*, on the Settings page, saves all your texts, and your versions of published texts, in one file.
- *Import a file…*, under *New* or on the Settings page, reads either kind of file. A text is added to *My texts*. An export brings back the texts it holds, and its versions of published texts replace the ones in this browser.

## How long texts are kept

Everything stays until the browser's data for this site is cleared. A private window starts empty and forgets everything when it is closed, and another browser or device has its own, separate copy.

*Erase everything saved in this browser*, on the Settings page, removes your texts, edits and settings. It takes two clicks.
