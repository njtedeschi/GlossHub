# How the site works

GlossHub shows a text line by line, with its analysis around each line: glosses, scansion, a transliteration, notes and more, each of which you can show or hide. Texts are written in a plain-text format, which this guide explains.

## Texts

The *Texts* page has two tabs: *Published texts*, which come with the site, and *My texts*, the ones you write, import or copy. Your texts, and your edits to published ones, are saved in this browser (see [Saving and exporting](#guide-saving-and-exporting)).

## Reading and editing

Every text has its own page with a **Read | Edit** switch:

- **Read** shows the text with your display options.
- **Edit** lets you click a gloss, translation or note to change it, or open the text's source to change anything else (see [Editing a text](#guide-editing-a-text)).

*Display options*, above the text, choose what you see (see [Display options](#guide-display-options)).

## Trying the examples

Every example in this guide is live. It shows the source, which you write, and the result, which the page shows. Press *Edit this example* to change the source; the result updates as you type, and *Reset* brings the original back. Changes to an example aren't saved.

Examples are always shown with the same display options, whatever yours are, so that each shows what it is about.
