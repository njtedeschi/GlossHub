# About

GlossHub is a tool for annotating texts in linguistically interesting ways, such as interlinear glosses, scansion, and notes, at the level of detail each reader chooses.

## What it does

- Displays a text line by line with word-aligned, interlinear annotations: transliteration, morphemic glosses (following the [Leipzig Glossing Rules](https://www.eva.mpg.de/lingua/resources/glossing-rules.php)), informal word-by-word glosses, parts of speech, and IPA transcriptions.
- Additionally allows for free translations and notes that can be associated to words, word groupings, or the text as a whole.
- Marks scansion over the syllables of verse, and ruby readings over characters.
- Handles right-to-left scripts such as Hebrew, and long lines split into sublines.
- Lets each reader choose what to see: every row can be shown, hidden or put in another order.
- Lets anyone edit a text cell by cell (note: this feature is still in progress), or write one from scratch in a plain-text format.

## How it works

Texts are written in a plain-text format, one line at a time; the [Guide](#guide) explains it topic by topic. GlossHub runs entirely in your browser: there is no account and no server.

## Your texts and your privacy

- Your own texts, your edits to published texts and your settings are saved in this browser, on this device, and nowhere else. Nobody else can see them.
- They stay until the browser's data for this site is cleared. A private window starts empty and forgets everything when it is closed, and another browser or device has its own, separate copy.
- To keep a copy, or move your texts to another browser, use **Export everything** in [Settings](#settings), and import the file where you want them. Each text can also be exported from its page.
- GlossHub collects nothing about you: no analytics, no cookies, no tracking. The fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them, as it would on many sites.
